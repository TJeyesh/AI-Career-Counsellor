Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this aptitude test?

Our test is based on the scientifically-validated RIASEC model developed by psychologist John Holland. It's used worldwide for career guidance. While no test is 100% accurate, this provides valuable insights into your personality and career fit.

Can I change my stream later?

Yes, but it's easier to choose the right stream initially. Some changes are possible (like Commerce to Arts), but switching to Science stream later is difficult because it requires PCM/PCB background.

Do I need to register to use this platform?

No! Our platform is completely free and doesn't require registration. However, you may want to save your results for future reference.

How long does the assessment take?

The complete assessment takes about 10-15 minutes. There are 24 questions covering different aspects of your personality, interests, and preferences.
